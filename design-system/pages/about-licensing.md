# Page override: Licensing by state (`/about/licensing/`)
Inherits `design-system/MASTER.md`. Only deviations are listed.

- **Purpose:** the agency licence number for every state. Load bearing: the shared footer's licence disclosure links here from all seventeen pages, so this is the one that has to be right.
- **The table is the page.** No hero photograph and no lead card: there is no argument to make here, only a list to publish. `.table-scroll.table-signature` directly under the H1 and its `.flag`.
- **The flag sits above the table, not below it.** A reader who takes a number off this page must meet the placeholder notice before the number, not after.
- **Dated `.pill` beneath** carries the National Producer Number and the statement that a state's own lookup is authoritative over this page.
- **Rows are deletions, not edits.** Populate from current records and delete every unused row. A state listed here that the agency cannot write in misstates the footprint, which is worse than listing too few.
- **`what a licence does and does not tell you`** is a two-cell bento, white beside tint, at deliberately equal weight. The second cell says a licence is a floor rather than a recommendation.
- Layout families in order: hero + flag + table, split + bento pair, accordion, navy band. Eyebrow budget 2, used 2.
