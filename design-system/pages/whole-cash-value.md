# /whole-life-insurance/cash-value/

Deviations from MASTER.md. Everything not listed here is inherited.

**Soft CTA only. No amber anywhere on this page.** The audience is financially literate and is
actively comparing this product against investing the difference. The spec is explicit that hard
selling this reader loses them. There is no form, no `btn-cta`, and one ask near the end: a ghost
button reading "Request a policy illustration" beside a plain phone link.

**The buy-term-and-invest-the-difference section is written to be genuinely balanced, not
balanced-looking.** Two equal columns, "where the argument is right" first. If it reads as a
rebuttal it has failed, because this reader has already heard the rebuttal and can tell. The four
points in the left column are real concessions and must not be softened.

**The cash-value chart is `whole.cash_value_chart()`, reused verbatim.** This page is its canonical
home; the hub carries the other copy. A second, richer chart here would give two pages competing
signature objects for one intent. It carries no dollar amounts on either axis, is labelled
illustrative on its face, and its `stroke-dashoffset` draw-in collapses under
`prefers-reduced-motion` like every other motion on the site.

**The tax section's `[GENERAL INFORMATION ONLY]` flag is permanent.** It is a caveat, not a
placeholder, and it stays after launch. Every claim in that section is hedged ("generally", "under
current federal rules", "for a policy that is not a modified endowment contract") and the flag names
consulting a tax professional. Do not tighten that language into advice.

**Borrowing gets the same space as growth.** Four numbered steps ending in "the policy can collapse
under the loan", which is where these policies quietly fail and is the section most competitors
omit.
