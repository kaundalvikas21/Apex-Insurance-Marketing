# Page override: Home (`/`)
Inherits `design-system/MASTER.md`. Only deviations are listed.

- **Purpose:** brand and triage. Links to the three hubs, never to spokes.
- **Hero:** `.glow`. Four elements: h1, lead, one link line, photograph (`home-hero`, the page's
  one eager image). The three product paths sit directly beneath as a three-cell bento
  (`.bento-2` x3, middle cell tinted), 40ms stagger, each carrying its silo's CTA weighting.
- **Comparison:** the signature section. Four-cell bento: three product stat cells (white, tinted,
  blue) with one count-up spec figure each (30 yrs, "Lifelong" static, 15 min) and their row-level
  links, then a `.bento-6` signature comparison table. No `tfoot`; the links live in the cells so
  no target is linked twice.
- **Independence band:** solid navy, no photograph, no scrim. The agent card is a raised
  white-at-8% surface, not glass.
- **Final CTA:** two hover cards, not a divided row.
- Layout families in order: hero + bento, strip, form panel, step trio, navy band, bento + table,
  accordion, card pair. Eyebrow budget 3, used 3 (inside the comparison cells).
