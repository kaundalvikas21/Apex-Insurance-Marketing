Is this free?

Yes. Quotes, comparisons, and the conversation cost you nothing. If you buy a policy, the carrier pays us a commission out of the premium you would have paid anyway. Life insurance rates are filed with state regulators and are the same wherever you buy. You never pay Apex a fee.

Am I obligated to buy anything?

No. Asking for a quote commits you to nothing, and neither does completing an application. Nothing is owed and no coverage exists until a policy is issued, delivered, and the first premium is paid. You can stop at any point without explaining why.

Who will contact me?

A licensed agent from Apex Insurance Marketing, LLC, and nobody else. We do not sell, rent, or share your details with other agencies, lead buyers, or marketing partners. You will not start getting calls from numbers you do not recognize. That is what happens on comparison sites that sell the same inquiry to several agencies at once.
